Gait Review

Record a short run, then play it back frame by frame with the skeleton, joint angles and coaching notes on top. Nothing is shown over the camera while you run.

  1. Prop the phone about 3 m away at hip height. Side-on for stride mechanics, directly behind for hip and knee alignment.
  2. Tap record, then run normally. A three-second countdown gives you time to settle.
  3. Stop and review: scrub the clip, step a frame at a time, and jump between foot contacts.

The clip is held in memory for this session only and is never written to your photo library or device storage; starting a new recording discards the previous one. About 2 MB per 10 seconds. Live coaching mode →
