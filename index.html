Gait Coach

Real-time running form analysis for the treadmill. Pose tracking runs entirely on this phone; no video leaves the device.

  1. Prop the phone about 3 m from the treadmill, at hip height, in portrait. Side-on for stride mechanics, directly behind for hip and knee alignment.
  2. Level it. The horizon line and level readout go green when the camera is straight. Angles are auto-corrected for small tilts.
  3. Frame the whole runner, head to feet, and start running. Cues appear on screen and are spoken after a few strides.

Reference ranges come from published 2D running-analysis literature for healthy recreational runners. This is a coaching aid, not a medical device; single-camera estimates carry a few degrees of uncertainty.
